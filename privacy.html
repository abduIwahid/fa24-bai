Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

FA24-BAI is an educational resource hub for university students. We do not require account registration or collect personal tracking data from general visitors. When utilizing the AI assistant or downloading files, standard technical session headers are processed solely to deliver serverless functions.

2. Local Storage and Preferences

This application stores limited operational data locally on your device using browser LocalStorage and SessionStorage, including:

  • Notice read receipts to keep track of unread announcements.
  • Session authentication credentials for administrative resource uploads.
  • Recently accessed documents for quick offline recall.

3. Third-Party Services

To deliver course materials and study aids, this platform integrates with:

  • GitHub API: Acts as the primary repository for course documents and syllabus sheets.
  • Google Generative AI (Gemini): Powers contextual document summaries and quiz generation when requested.
  • Microsoft Office Web Viewer: Provides read-only browser rendering for PowerPoint and Word files.

4. Data Security

Administrative actions such as file uploads and deletions are guarded by server-side verification keys. No private student communications or academic transcripts are stored on our servers.

5. Contact and Inquiries

For questions regarding this privacy policy or to request resource revisions, please open an issue in the official FA24-BAI repository.
